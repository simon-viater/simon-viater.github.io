About

MSc Financial Economics at Erasmus University Rotterdam, after a BSc in Economics & Business Administration at Aarhus University and an exchange at HKUST. I am an energetic fast-learner and an outside-the-box thinker. For a year I was a financial assistant at IMMOCAP, one of Slovakia’s largest real estate developers: reporting on the firm’s debt structure to shareholders, comparing bank term sheets for project financing, maintaining rent rolls for valuation and automating pricing templates. The models here are my own case studies, built to keep those muscles working.

When I’m not diving into financial data, you’ll find me rock climbing, surfing, traveling, or jamming on my ukulele.
